BEAUTY SLEEP — ALL ADS
======================

This folder is a portable, offline gallery containing 45 finished ads:

- 10 Husband ads
- 5 Mums ads
- 10 Dubai dry-mouth ads
- 10 Menopause ads
- 10 Snoring ads

HOW TO VIEW
-----------

1. Keep this entire folder together.
2. Double-click index.html.
3. The gallery will open in your normal web browser.

You can search, filter by audience/problem/angle, change how ads are grouped,
view ads at full size, and download any original PNG.

HOW TO SHARE
------------

Send the "All Ads.zip" file. Your teammate can unzip it and double-click
index.html. The gallery does not require an internet connection or a website
login.

Do not move index.html out of this folder by itself; it needs the assets folder
beside it in order to display the ads.
